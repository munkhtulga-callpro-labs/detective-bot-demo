Generating 100 fully detailed, multi-layered mystery webs with timelines, motives, and physics breakdowns in a single response would crush the token limit and result in shallow, repetitive ideas.
Instead, to give you a massive, highly diverse library you can actually use to build your game, I have designed 100 Unique Crime Scene Seeds.
To maximize diversity, they are categorized into 4 Genres (25 cases each). Each seed is presented as a high-concept Logline + The Hidden Logic Twist that you can feed directly into an AI system prompt to generate the full case file.
